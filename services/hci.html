Service 01

Hyper-Converged
Infrastructure

Architecture, deployment, scaling, and migration across Nutanix AHV, VMware vSphere/vSAN, and Cisco HyperFlex — from greenfield builds to complex multi-site expansions and VMware alternative planning.

What we deliver

Greenfield HCI architecture — cluster sizing, node selection, network design, and storage policy definition for new deployments on Nutanix AHV or VMware vSphere.

VMware to Nutanix migration — workload assessment, migration planning, and live VM migration using Nutanix Move — minimising downtime on production environments.

Multi-site and DR cluster design — replication topology, stretched cluster architecture, and Metro Availability configuration for high-availability across sites.

Cluster scaling and expansion — node-add procedures, storage expansion, and rolling upgrade planning without service disruption.

VMware vSphere and vSAN upgrades — version planning, pre-checks, and upgrade execution across multi-host clusters from vSphere 6.x through to current versions.

Broadcom licensing migration assessment — independent analysis of your current VMware estate and a clear comparison of migration paths: Nutanix, OpenShift, hybrid cloud, or renegotiated VMware.

PoC design and execution — structured proof-of-concept engagements for HCI procurement decisions, with documented test plans and evaluation criteria.

Cisco HyperFlex — design, deployment, and support for HyperFlex all-flash and hybrid configurations.
