Why infrastructure expertise matters for AI

Most AI projects fail at the infrastructure layer, not the model layer.

Data scientists build the models. The failure point is almost always underneath — storage that can't handle vector embeddings at scale, networking that creates latency, compute that wasn't sized for inference workloads, or compliance requirements that weren't considered until go-live.

Our advantage is that we design the AI layer and the infrastructure layer together — from the same practitioner who has deployed Nutanix, OpenShift, and enterprise storage across Kuwait banking and government for 12 years.
