Virtualisation
& VDI

Virtual desktop and application delivery at enterprise scale. VMware Horizon, Citrix CVAD, NSX-T network virtualisation, vSAN, App Volumes, and UEM — from 50 seats to 500+ across banking and government environments.

What we deliver

VMware Horizon VDI — end-to-end design, deployment, and upgrade of Horizon environments including Connection Server, Unified Access Gateway, App Volumes, and Dynamic Environment Manager.

Citrix CVAD / XenDesktop — Citrix Virtual Apps and Desktops architecture, deployment, and integration with Citrix ADC (NetScaler) for secure remote access.

NSX-T network virtualisation — micro-segmentation, distributed firewall, and software-defined networking for virtualised environments — reducing east-west attack surface.

vSphere upgrades and migrations — structured upgrade paths from vSphere 6.x to current versions across multi-host clusters with minimal service disruption.

Hyper-V to AHV migration — VM migration from Microsoft Hyper-V to Nutanix AHV, including workload assessment and cut-over planning.

vRealize Operations — monitoring, capacity planning, and performance optimisation for VMware environments using vROps.
