OpenShift platform architecture on VMware ESXi and Nutanix AHV — from initial container strategy through to production, pre-production, and DR cluster deployment. Bridging traditional enterprise infrastructure with cloud-native delivery.

What we deliver

Container platform strategy — assessing organisational readiness, workload suitability, and the right architecture model (IPI vs UPI) before a line of configuration is written.

OpenShift on VMware ESXi — IPI and UPI deployment on existing VMware infrastructure, including vSphere CSI driver integration, load balancer configuration, and day-2 operations setup.

OpenShift on Nutanix AHV — IPI deployment leveraging Nutanix CSI for persistent volumes, Nutanix Flow microsegmentation, and Prism Central management integration.

Multi-environment cluster design — separate production, pre-production, and DR cluster architecture with appropriate resource allocation, RBAC, and network separation.

Storage class design — persistent volume strategy, storage class definitions, and data protection via CSI snapshots — not just VM-level backup.

Network policy and security — OVN-Kubernetes configuration, ingress design, and network policy implementation for workload isolation.

Workload migration planning — assessment of existing applications for containerisation suitability and migration sequencing from VM-based to container-based delivery.
