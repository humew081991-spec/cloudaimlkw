What we deliver

DR strategy design — workload tiering, RPO/RTO target setting, and technology selection aligned to what replication platforms can realistically deliver.

Veeam Backup & Replication — design, implementation, and optimisation including Scale-Out Backup Repository, SureBackup, and immutable storage targets.

Zerto continuous replication — journal-based replication for tier-1 workloads requiring sub-15-minute RPO, including non-disruptive failover testing.

Immutable backup architecture — IBM FlashSystem immutable target design and Veeam hardened repository configuration — ransomware-resistant backup chains.

Veritas NetBackup — implementation, upgrade, and optimisation for enterprise environments including backup window reduction and deduplication tuning.

Enterprise archiving — Veritas Enterprise Vault design for Exchange, file server, and SharePoint archiving with eDiscovery capability and compliance retention policies.

DR testing programme — documented failover test plans, quarterly test scheduling, and test result documentation — turning a theoretical DR plan into a proven one.
