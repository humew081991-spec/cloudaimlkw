End-to-end identity modernisation — on-premises Active Directory to Microsoft Entra ID. Hybrid identity design, Entra Connect synchronisation, conditional access governance, and legacy authentication remediation.

What we deliver

Identity assessment and migration planning — full discovery of your AD environment, UPN suffix audit, legacy authentication dependency mapping, and phased migration roadmap.

Entra Connect design and deployment — synchronisation scope, OU filtering, attribute flow configuration, and password hash synchronisation vs pass-through authentication selection.

Conditional access policy design — staged policy rollout from Report-Only through to enforced MFA, device compliance, and location-based controls — without locking out legitimate users.

Legacy authentication remediation — identification and remediation of applications, devices, and services using NTLM, Basic Auth, and other modern-auth-incompatible protocols.

Active Directory restructuring — domain consolidation, OU design, Group Policy rationalisation, FSMO role placement, and AD topology cleanup prior to Entra sync.

Microsoft 365 and Exchange migrations — Exchange 2010/2013/2016 to Exchange Online, mailbox migration, and SharePoint Online deployment.

PKI and certificate services — Public Key Infrastructure design, implementation, and migration — including AD Certificate Services and Kerberos authentication.
