Case study 01 · Banking sector

OpenShift on Nutanix — Production, Pre-Prod & DR

A Kuwaiti bank needed to modernise application delivery and reduce dependency on traditional VM-based infrastructure, while maintaining the strict availability requirements of a financial institution.

The challenge

The bank's development teams were ready for containerised workloads, but the infrastructure team had no OpenShift experience. The requirement was for three fully isolated environments — production, pre-production, and DR — on existing Nutanix nodes.

The solution

IPI-based OpenShift deployment across three cluster environments on Nutanix AHV. Nutanix CSI for persistent storage, OVN-Kubernetes for network policy, and a structured knowledge transfer programme for the internal team.

The outcome

Three isolated OpenShift environments live within project timeline

Internal team fully trained and operating independently post-handover

Existing Nutanix investment leveraged — no additional hardware required

Case study 02 · Enterprise sector

Active Directory to Microsoft Entra ID Migration

A large Kuwait enterprise needed to modernise its identity infrastructure, improve security posture with MFA, and reduce dependence on ageing on-premises Active Directory servers.

The challenge

A complex legacy AD environment with multiple OUs, dozens of applications using legacy authentication protocols, and no prior cloud identity experience in the IT team. Previous migration attempts had stalled due to undiscovered application dependencies.

The solution

Full legacy authentication dependency audit, AD restructuring, Entra Connect deployment with password hash synchronisation, and a phased conditional access rollout starting in Report-Only mode.

The outcome

Zero authentication outages during cutover

MFA enforced across all users within 6 weeks of project start

Legacy authentication fully blocked — attack surface significantly reduced

Case study 03 · Government sector

HCI Deployment with Full DR Architecture

A Kuwait government entity needed to consolidate ageing server infrastructure, establish a proper disaster recovery capability, and reduce the operational burden on a small internal IT team.

The challenge

A mix of ageing physical servers and an ad-hoc virtualisation environment with no documented DR plan. The entity had experienced extended downtime from a previous hardware failure with no recovery capability in place.

The solution

Nutanix HCI production cluster with a separate DR site, Nutanix replication for near-zero RPO on critical workloads, and Veeam for backup — with a tested and documented recovery runbook.

The outcome

Server footprint reduced by 60% through consolidation

Recovery time reduced from days to under 2 hours

First successful DR test completed within 30 days of go-live
