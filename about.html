Cloud AI ML Consulting is an independent infrastructure and cloud advisory practice based in Kuwait — built on 12 years of hands-on architecture and delivery across the Gulf region's most demanding IT environments.

The practice

Cloud AI ML Consulting was founded on a straightforward premise: large enterprises and government organisations in Kuwait deserve the same quality of infrastructure architecture advice that used to be available only through global system integrators — but delivered by someone who is genuinely accountable, vendor-independent, and present throughout the engagement.

The practice draws on 12 years of infrastructure delivery across Kuwait's banking sector, government ministries, healthcare, energy, logistics, retail, and hospitality — covering the full stack from hyper-converged compute and enterprise storage through to virtualisation, containers, cloud, identity, and disaster recovery.

Operating as a boutique practice means every engagement receives senior attention. There are no junior teams, no hand-offs, and no diluted advice filtered through account management layers. The architect who scopes your project designs it, delivers it, and hands it over.

The current focus areas reflect what the Kuwait and Gulf market needs most right now: Red Hat OpenShift as organisations shift workloads to containerised platforms, Microsoft Entra ID migration as hybrid identity becomes the standard, and VMware alternative architecture as Broadcom licensing disruption forces infrastructure re-evaluation.

The longer-term ambition is to grow Cloud AI ML Consulting into a regional system integrator — bringing in specialist subcontractors for delivery capacity while maintaining the boutique advisory quality that differentiates the practice today.

Why vendor-independent matters

"The best infrastructure for your organisation is the one that fits your workloads, budget, and operating model — not the one that generates the most margin for a reseller."

What this means in practice

Recommendations across Nutanix, VMware, Pure Storage, IBM, Veeam, and others are made on technical merit and fit — not on partner status or resale margin. You get an honest comparison, including the option to stay with what you have if that's genuinely the right answer.

Where this practice is different

Large system integrators carry vendor quotas, renewal targets, and sales pressure. A boutique independent practice has none of those constraints — which means your architecture is designed around your requirements, not around hitting a vendor's FY target.
