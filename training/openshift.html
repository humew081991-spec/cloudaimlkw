Topics covered

Container Fundamentals

What containers are, how they differ from VMs, and why the industry is moving toward them. Docker, Podman, and container images explained for infrastructure engineers.

Kubernetes Architecture

Kubernetes core concepts — pods, deployments, services, namespaces, and ingress — mapped to VMware and infrastructure concepts you already know.

OpenShift Administration

OpenShift-specific operations — projects, routes, operators, image streams, and the OpenShift web console. Day-2 operations and upgrade procedures.

OpenShift on Nutanix

Deploying OpenShift on Nutanix AHV using IPI. Nutanix CSI driver, storage classes, Prism integration, and operational patterns for production clusters.

OpenShift on VMware

OpenShift on VMware vSphere — IPI deployment, vSphere CSI, NSX-T integration, and managing OpenShift alongside existing VMware infrastructure.

From VMware Admin to OpenShift Engineer

The career transition programme — using your existing infrastructure knowledge to accelerate OpenShift proficiency. Networking, storage, and operations mapped across both platforms.

Delivery formats

Live Online

Who this is for

VMware and infrastructure administrators transitioning to OpenShift, IT teams operating OpenShift in production, engineers who want to move into DevOps and platform engineering roles.
