Nutanix Training

Topics covered

Nutanix HCI Fundamentals

Nutanix architecture, cluster components, storage controller (CVM), and how AHV compares to VMware ESXi. Ideal starting point for teams new to Nutanix.

Prism Central & Prism Element

Daily administration through Prism — VM management, storage containers, alerts, cluster health, and reporting. Hands-on workflows your team will use every day.

AHV Hypervisor Administration

Nutanix AHV operations — VM creation, networking, snapshots, migration with Nutanix Move, and AHV vs ESXi operational differences.

Storage Policies & Data Protection

Storage container configuration, replication factor, erasure coding, deduplication, compression, and snapshot-based data protection.

Nutanix Replication & DR

Nutanix replication for disaster recovery — Protection Domains, Recovery Plans, and Metro Availability for near-zero RPO across sites.

NCP Exam Preparation

Structured preparation for the Nutanix Certified Professional (NCP-MCI) exam — covering all exam domains with practice scenarios and exam technique.

IT teams deploying or operating Nutanix environments, VMware administrators transitioning to Nutanix AHV, engineers preparing for NCP or NCM certification.
