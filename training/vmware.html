Topics covered

vSphere Administration

Install, configure, and manage VMware vSphere environments. vCenter Server, ESXi hosts, VM management, vMotion, DRS, HA, and day-to-day operations.

NSX-T Network Virtualisation

Software-defined networking and micro-segmentation with VMware NSX-T. Distributed firewall, overlay networking, and east-west traffic control.

VMware Horizon VDI

Virtual desktop and application delivery with VMware Horizon. Connection Server, Unified Access Gateway, App Volumes, and Dynamic Environment Manager.

vSAN Storage

VMware vSAN architecture, configuration, storage policies, and performance tuning. Sizing, deduplication, and upgrade procedures.

VMware to Nutanix Migration

Planning and executing a migration from VMware to Nutanix AHV. Workload assessment, Nutanix Move, and cutover strategies for production environments.

VMware Upgrades

Structured upgrade paths from vSphere 6.x to current versions. Pre-checks, upgrade sequencing, and rollback planning for multi-host clusters.
